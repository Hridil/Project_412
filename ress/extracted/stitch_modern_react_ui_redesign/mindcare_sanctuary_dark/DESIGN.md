## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tranquil, restorative environment tailored for clinical psychiatric and psychological digital care. Designed specifically as a dedicated dark-mode ecosystem, it rejects harsh stark contrasts and clinical sterility in favor of organic sanctuary spaces. It balances quiet reassurance with clinical authority, providing patients and clinicians a safe, grounding interface during moments of vulnerability and focus.

The aesthetic blends **Organic Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep submerged night surfaces bathed in deep spruce teal tones that prevent eye fatigue and reduce anxiety.
- **Warmth & Grounding:** Delicate honey and amber accents introduce empathy, vitality, and clinical sanctuary warmth without aggressive stimulation.
- **Clarity & Dignity:** High editorial polish through timeless serif headings paired with clean, accessible sans-serif body text ensures high legibility under varying states of cognitive load.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for deep nocturnal reassurance, avoiding pitch-black starkness in favor of layered spruce and obsidian undertones:

- **Base Surfaces:**
  - `Surface 0 (Canvas)`: `#081414` — Deep night obsidian base.
  - `Surface 1 (Base Cards/Panels)`: `#0d1f1e` — Submerged spruce foundation.
  - `Surface 2 (Elevated Containers)`: `#132b2a` — Muted teal-tinted elevation tier.
  - `Surface 3 (Overlays/Floating)`: `#1a3836` — Focused interactive background.
- **Accents & Highlights:**
  - `Primary Teal Accent`: `#2dd4bf` — Luminous sea-glass teal for interactive controls, focus anchors, and safe progression.
  - `Primary Glow / Active`: `#5eead4` — High-clarity mint sheen for hover states and active indicators.
  - `Warm Honey Highlight`: `#f2c879` — Empathic amber accent for care alerts, milestones, and therapeutic callouts.
  - `Deep Honey Contrast`: `#e0a94e` — Grounded clinical anchor for badges and active toggles.
- **Typography & Structural Accents:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#e3efec` — Soft sage-white for high-legibility without retinal strain.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#b7d3ce` — Calming eucalyptus gray for metadata, instructions, and secondary copy.
  - `Text Muted / Subtle`: `#7e9e98` — Muted spruce for passive metadata, dividers, and disabled states.
  - `Border Soothing`: `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.14)` — Translucent oceanic framing.
  - `Border Focus`: `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.45)` — Reassuring active ring.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual-engine architecture:
1. **Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for display elements, clinical module titles, and therapeutic anchors. It communicates empathy, clinical prestige, human warmth, and grounding dignity. It should never be italicized aggressively or used in all-caps.
2. **Supportive Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Serves body copy, clinical form inputs, tracking metrics, and interaction labels. Its soft curves complement the serenity of the spruce surfaces while maintaining crisp legibility even under low backlighting.

Never decrease line heights below specified values; ample vertical breathability is mandatory to avoid cognitive claustrophobia in clinical care interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a fluid 12-column grid layout across desktop (max-width `1440px`), collapsing into an 8-column layout for tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Form modules and clinical diaries reflow strictly to single columns.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Secondary clinical sidebars collapse into bottom slide sheets.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12 columns, `margin` (2.5rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Centered workspace with spacious breathing corridors.

### Spatial Rhythm
Vertical rhythm prioritizes breathing room. Use `space-2xl` between distinct clinical functional zones (e.g., patient vital trends vs. therapy notes), and rely on `space-md` through `space-lg` inside cards to foster calm, unhurried navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **Tonal Surface Stacking** accented with **Subtle Emerald/Teal Bioluminescent Glows**, deliberately eschewing heavy black drop-shadows.

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#081414` (Deep Night Obsidian).
   - **Level 1 (Structural Content/Cards):** `#0d1f1e` with subtle outline `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.10)`.
   - **Level 2 (Active/Hovered Containers):** `#132b2a` with outline `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.18)` and an ambient aura: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.06)`.
   - **Level 3 (Modals, Clinical Prompts, Drawers):** `#1a3836` bordered with `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.28)` and elevated by dual glow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(3, 10, 10, 0.75), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.10)`.

2. **Ambient Glow Rule:**
   Shadows are always tinted with teal or obsidian values—never pure grey or desaturated black. Critical insights or active calming states can emit a muted honey ambient glow using `0 0 24px -2px rgba(242, 200, 121, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, soft geometry corresponding to **Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard UI containers, cards, and input modules adopt `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger restorative cards, clinical dialogue modules, and hero containers use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Modals, prominent treatment pathways, and wellness progress panels utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Interactive pills, emotion selector chips, and avatars are fully round (`9999px`).

Avoid sharp corners; softer radii reduce tension and promote a safe psychological space.
