## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Emotional Narrative
This design system represents a thoughtful, grounded approach to behavioral health practice management. It merges clinical precision with therapeutic tranquility, rejecting the sterile, utilitarian aesthetic of traditional healthcare software in favor of an environment that radiates emotional grounding, quiet capability, and trust. The atmosphere evokes a well-appointed, serene consultation room: organic sage and deep forest greens convey natural stillness; cream-tinted canvases eliminate screen glare; and precise slate framing gives structure to complex clinical workflows.

The interface serves two distinct user perspectives:
1. **Practitioners and Clinical Administrators:** Seeking cognitive clarity, reduced documentation fatigue, and swift, calm control over scheduling, clinical records, and patient continuity.
2. **Clients and Patients:** Seeking comfort, privacy, and frictionless access to appointments, self-reflection prompts, and treatment goals.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Editorial Healthcare SaaS**. It bridges the warmth and intellectual authority of classic editorial publishing with modern SaaS ergonomics. Literary serif typography commands structural milestones, while human-centered sans-serif type handles high-density administrative operations. Depth is constructed through quiet surface delineation, hairline slate borders, and subtle tactile shifts rather than dramatic elevation, producing a calm, distraction-free environment.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is rooted in botanical tones, mineral surfaces, and warm highlights, crafted to support prolonged clinical review and reduce eye strain.

- **Primary (`#0f766e` / `teal-700`):** The primary interactive anchor. Directs clinical focus toward vital actions—confirming session notes, submitting treatment plans, and navigating primary workflows. In dark mode, it elevates to luminous seafoam (`#2dd4bf`) for accessible contrast against deep obsidian surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0c3b37`):** Deep botanical forest. Used for structural foundations, persistent navigation panels, authentication hero panels, and authoritative branding.
- **Tertiary (`#e07a5f`):** Earthy terracotta. Used sparingly for critical patient milestones, active alerts, and warm metric indicators requiring immediate practitioner awareness.
- **Neutral (`#5f7571`):** Calibrated slate-gray with a jade undertone. Drives structural borders, muted clinical metadata, empty-state placeholders, and table headers without visual dissonance.

### Functional Chromatic Application
- **Surface Layering:** Avoid stark `#000000` or `#ffffff` pairings. Light mode rests on a mineral foundation (`#f2f5f3`) with pure `#ffffff` cards. Dark mode operates across an obsidian-teal foundation (`#0d1b1a`) layered with deep spruce containers (`#14292a`).
- **Data & Metric Accents:** Key metrics leverage dedicated category tints: Terracotta (`#e07a5f`) for acute client alerts, Warm Gold (`#e9b44c`) for pending consultations, and Slate Blue (`#6c8ebf`) for longitudinal goals and scheduling tracks.
- **Pastel Status Systems:** State alerts rely on soft pill backgrounds with high-contrast, deeply saturated text tones, maintaining WCAG AAA legibility while avoiding jarring alarms.

## Typography

### Typographic Harmony
The typographic system pairs an authoritative, humanistic serif with a precise, geometry-informed sans-serif.

- **Headline Font (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers warmth, gravitas, and literary presence. Reserved for milestone headings, module titles, page anchors, and clinical metric tallies. It communicates stability and care, counterbalancing the density of data-heavy schedules and clinical charts.
- **Body & Label Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Handles all high-density interfaces, session logs, notes, patient demographics, form fields, and status indicators. Its open aperture and balanced x-height ensure complete legibility under intense operational use.

### Editorial Hierarchies & Legibility
- **Table Column Headers:** Fixed at `11px`, transformed to uppercase with `0.05em` letter tracking to provide crisp, quiet categorical labeling over complex rows.
- **Clinical Numerics:** Key metric values employ `Playfair Display` at `30px`, introducing an organic, human touch to clinical data visualization.
- **Line Heights:** Body text defaults to a relaxed `1.6` line-height ratio, prioritizing reading comfort for long-form therapy consultation notes and intake narratives.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The architecture balances structured density for practitioners with spacious breathing room for patient check-ins. The operational core relies on a fixed-width left rail (`250px`) coupled with a fluid, centered workspace bound by a strict maximum container (`1300px`).

### Spatial Hierarchy & Grid Models
- **Master Canvas Margins:** Desktop viewports use a generous `1.75rem` (`28px`) margin, tightening to `1rem` (`16px`) on mobile viewports.
- **Module Grid Gutters:** The primary dashboard utilizes an 18px (`1.125rem`) gutter model, balancing content separation with ergonomic field proximity.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed left navigation rail (`250px`), 12-column grid workspace, top sticky clinical status bar, horizontal split widgets for scheduling and recent chart feeds.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Collapsed navigation rail to icon state or modal flyout drawer, dual-column responsive card arrangement, table views with horizontal overflow protection.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout. Navigation shifts to a persistent drawer governed by a `z-20` overlay. Padding contracts across the board (`space-md` on cards, `space-sm` on input fields).

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Depth Strategy
Visual hierarchy relies on mineral layering and micro-elevation rather than heavy drop shadows. Surfaces feel clean, level, and tactile.

- **Surface Tiers (Light Mode):**
  - Level 0 (Canvas Base): `#f2f5f3` mineral backdrop.
  - Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation): Pure `#ffffff` surface delineated by a `1px` solid `#dde7e3` hairline border.
  - Level 2 (Interactive Floating Controls): `#ffffff` backed by an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(12, 59, 55, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(12, 59, 55, 0.02)`.
  - Level 3 (Overlays & Drawers): Supported by `z-20` scrims (`rgba(8, 31, 30, 0.4)`) and ambient soft projection: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(8, 31, 30, 0.15)`.

- **Surface Tiers (Dark Mode):**
  - Level 0 (Canvas Base): Deep obsidian teal `#0d1b1a`.
  - Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation): Deep forest slate `#14292a` framed with `#23403f` borders.
  - Shadows in dark mode are stripped completely, relying exclusively on hairline contrast boundaries and soft tint highlights.

### Layering Z-Index Scale
- `z-10`: Sticky top operational header, preserving location context during long chart audits.
- `z-20`: Persistent mobile slide-over navigation rail and filter sheets.
- `z-30`: Floating toast notifications, system-level crisis warnings, and real-time appointment alerts.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
The interface uses soft, geometric radii to cultivate an approachable, calming visual environment while preserving the architectural order expected of clinical software.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Selects:** Form elements, standard buttons, and segmented control tabs feature precise `10px` rounding, creating comfortable, defined targets for clinical input.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** Dashboard cards, clinical charts, and diagnostic widgets use `16px` (`rounded-2xl`) corners to softly delineate workspaces.
- **Mood Cards & Action Tiles:** Interactive sentiment selection items use `12px` (`rounded-xl`) corners.
- **Pills, Avatars, and Badges:** Status badges, metric counters, avatar indicators, and interactive chip filters use a complete pill format (`9999px` / `rounded-full`), reinforcing their identity as dynamic metadata objects.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Built with `teal-700` (`#0f766e`) background, `#ffffff` label, `10px` border-radius, `py-[9px]` and `px-[15px]` padding. Hover state transitions within `150ms` to `#115e59`. In dark mode, background transitions to `#2dd4bf` with high-contrast `#052321` text.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface tint `#e3f1ee` (`dark:bg-[#173b3a]`), interactive text `#0b5a54` (`dark:text-[#5eead4]`), borderless or bordered by hairline `#dde7e3`.
- **Danger Action:** Grounded soft coral `#fde8e4` surface (`dark:bg-[#3b1f1b]`) with bold `#b3392a` text (`dark:text-[#f4a496]`). Avoids harsh red alarms while clearly signaling destructive flows.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact pill tokens (`py-0.5 px-2.5`, `rounded-full`, `text-xs font-bold`).
- **Pending / In Review:** Cream-gold surface `#fff3df` with `#8a5a12` text (`dark:bg-[#3a2f17]`, `dark:text-[#f2c879]`).
- **Accepted / Active:** Soft spearmint surface `#dff3e6` with `#1f6b3d` text (`dark:bg-[#173a27]`, `dark:text-[#8fdcae]`).
- **Urgent / Canceled:** Soft blush surface `#fde8e4` with `#b3392a` text (`dark:bg-[#3b1f1b]`, `dark:text-[#f4a496]`).

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#ffffff` container (`dark:bg-[#14292a]`) framed with `1px` `#dde7e3` (`dark:border-[#23403f]`).
- Vertical padding `py-2.5`, horizontal `px-3.5`, radius `10px`.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring using `teal-700` (`#0f766e` / `dark:ring-[#2dd4bf]`), clearing browser defaults and signaling active engagement.
- Input labels use `label-md` (`13px`, semi-bold) in `#14302c` (`dark:text-[#e3efec]`), positioned cleanly above fields with a `6px` gap.

### Cards & Dashboards Modules
- Clean `#ffffff` fill (`dark:bg-[#14292a]`), hairline border (`1px solid #dde7e3`, `dark:border-[#23403f]`), `16px` border-radius, standard `p-5` inner cushion.
- **KPI Stat Cards:** Accentuated by an intentional `4px` top border (`border-t-4`) keyed to data domain: Terracotta for patient load, Gold for reviews, Slate Blue for session continuity, Teal for practice health.
- Headers inside cards pair a `18px` serif title with an optional trailing pill-badge or icon action.

### List Rows & Data Tables
- Header cells styled with `label-table-header` (`11px`, tracking-wider, uppercase, `#5f7571`), `p-[11px]` cell padding.
- Data rows feature subtle bottom borders (`1px solid #dde7e3`, `dark:border-[#23403f]`, omitted on the last row). Hover rows transition to `#f2f5f3` (`dark:hover:bg-[#173b3a]/40`).

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Mood Selectors
- Mood radio elements use tactile `12px` rounded cards with `2px` borders, transitioning from subtle slate `#dde7e3` to bold `#0f766e` with `#e3f1ee` fill upon selection.
- Checkboxes feature `4px` rounded profiles, matching primary brand fills when selected.

### Therapeutic & Specialized Widgets
- **Guided Breathing Timer:** A soothing circular breathing guide featuring a 2-second keyframe pulse expansion (`animate-pulse`) with multi-step concentric rings tinted in translucent brand teal.
- **Toast Notifications:** Fixed bottom-right pills (`rounded-xl`, `z-30`, `py-3 px-5`) utilizing high-contrast inverted surfaces (`#14302c` with `#f2f5f3` copy in light mode) for immediate clinical confirmation.
